What to approve

  • Overall style: flat colour blocks, one darker shade per colour, no outlines, no gradients on the subject, chunky rounded helmet, bold diagonal sweeps.
  • Palette: team primary, off-white #F5EFE6, charcoal #24232B, plus the team's secondary/accent. Very dark teams get a pale visor seal, and rims in their stripe colour, so nothing disappears.
  • Helmet angle and framing, and blue versus team-tinted background.
  • Car angle, proportions and the cream card treatment.

# VERIFIED CIRCUIT CHARACTER

Use these as factual constraints on the normalized Formula Tycoon profiles.

## MELBOURNE

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- relatively fast modern layout
- requires a responsive/well-balanced chassis
- temporary circuit can be bumpy
- grip evolves significantly
- relatively low tyre severity
- meaningful fast-direction-change performance
- not fundamentally a stop-and-go circuit

Review existing tyre-management value if it is currently high.

---

## SHANGHAI

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- long straights
- 1.2 km back straight
- substantial heavy braking
- very long sustained corners
- fast Turns 7–8 sequence
- traction important from slow corners
- notable left-front tyre loading

Should reward a balanced car rather than one single characteristic.

---

## SUZUKA

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- very high-speed circuit
- continuous changes of direction
- major aerodynamic balance requirement
- high lateral tyre loads
- among the toughest tyre-energy tracks
- strong medium/high-speed corner emphasis
- technical precision important

High-speed aero and tyre demand should be among the highest in the game.

---

## MIAMI

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- three long straights
- top speeds above 350 km/h
- major braking events at Turns 11 and 17
- fast sweeping first sector
- technical slow chicane
- traction important onto long straights
- relatively low surface roughness and historically limited degradation

Strong straight-line + braking demands with meaningful aero and traction.

Do not overstate tyre severity.

---

## MONTREAL

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- classic stop-and-go layout
- several straights
- strong braking stability is crucial
- maximum traction is important
- many slower corners/chicanes
- low-abrasion surface
- significant track evolution

Braking and traction should be defining characteristics.

---

## MONACO

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- extremely tight and narrow
- overwhelmingly low-speed
- traction-heavy tyre loading
- very smooth surface
- traditionally low tyre degradation
- mechanical grip extremely important
- qualifying position extremely important
- overtaking extremely difficult

Do not model Monaco as a high tyre-wear circuit.

---

## BARCELONA-CATALUNYA

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- one of the most complete/balanced circuits
- many high-speed corners
- long loaded corners
- strong aero-balance test
- high lateral tyre loads
- abrasive asphalt
- significant thermal degradation
- front axle particularly stressed

Strong aero/high-speed/tyre-management circuit.

---

## SPIELBERG

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- fast
- significant elevation change
- stop-and-go character
- repeated braking and acceleration
- downhill braking challenges
- strong rear-tyre traction loading
- approximately 660 m elevation
- thermal rather than pure wear degradation

Braking and traction should matter strongly.

---

## SILVERSTONE

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- one of the fastest circuits
- very high lateral forces
- rapid high-speed direction changes
- high tyre severity
- front axle heavily stressed
- high-speed aerodynamic stability is critical

High-speed aero and tyre demand should sit near the top of the calendar.

---

## SPA-FRANCORCHAMPS

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- long straights
- very fast corners
- significant aero-efficiency compromise
- first sector strongly straight-line biased
- second sector more technical/medium-speed
- third sector flowing
- weather highly variable
- thermal tyre degradation can matter

Strong balanced combination of straight-line speed, high-speed performance and aero efficiency.

---

## BUDAPEST

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- short, twisty, kart-track style
- only one meaningful straight
- many direction changes
- traction crucial
- braking stability crucial
- relatively slow overall character
- low grip with substantial track evolution
- setup traditionally comparable to Monaco

Mechanical grip/traction/low-to-medium-speed demand should be high.

Straight-line demand should be low.

---

## ZANDVOORT

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- fast flowing layout
- numerous high-speed corners
- high downforce requirement
- heavily banked corners
- very high vertical/lateral tyre forces
- high tyre energy density
- narrow track
- limited straight-line emphasis
- sand/wind can reduce grip

High aero and tyre demand.

---

## MONZA

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- roughly 80% full throttle
- exceptionally high average speed
- very long straights
- maximum-speed focus
- huge braking events into chicanes
- comparatively low downforce requirement

Straight-line demand should be at or near maximum.

Braking should also be very high.

---

## MADRID / MADRING

HIGH confidence.

Madrid is NO LONGER provisional merely because source access failed.

Verified 2026 characteristics:

- hybrid street/permanent circuit
- 22 corners
- approximately 340 km/h maximum speeds
- very fast opening sector
- medium and low-speed sections afterward
- multiple heavy braking zones
- final sector includes multiple 90-degree-style corners
- significant elevation changes
- Turn 12 / La Monumental approximately 550 m long
- banking up to 24%
- very high vertical loading at the banked corner
- Pirelli ranks the circuit among the five most demanding on the calendar for tyre energy
- lateral loads comparable to Barcelona/Silverstone
- new asphalt is extremely smooth and initially relatively low-grip

Update Madrid from PROVISIONAL to CONFIRMED/HIGH CONFIDENCE for the current technical model.

Future balance refinement remains allowed, but basic technical character is now supported.

---

## BAKU

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- approximately 2.2 km main straight
- extreme straight-line requirement
- major braking zones
- many slow and medium-speed street corners
- significant compromise between low drag and corner performance
- relatively low-abrasion surface
- narrow castle section
- low/medium-speed performance still matters despite straight length

This must remain a distinctive high-speed + heavy-braking + slow-corner hybrid.

---

## SEPANG

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- long straights
- middle sector strongly focused on high-speed balance and flow
- demanding high-speed Turns 5–6 sequence
- technical braking approaches at Turns 11 and 14
- traction exits important
- significant overtaking/braking opportunity at final corner
- extreme heat
- high humidity
- sudden heavy rain historically characteristic

High-speed/aero efficiency, cooling and weather metadata should matter strongly.

Do not make it merely another straight-line circuit.

---

## SINGAPORE

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- street circuit
- numerous braking and traction phases
- limited overtaking
- medium-to-high roughness for a street circuit
- substantial track evolution
- intermittent equatorial rainfall
- hot/humid night conditions
- rear axle performance loss is primarily traction-related
- Pirelli explicitly states mechanical stress is NOT especially high in 2026
- tyre overheating/degradation is not unusually limiting under current conditions

IMPORTANT:

If current Formula Tycoon data gives Singapore very high:

- reliability/car stress
- tyre-management severity

review those values downward.

Cooling/environmental demand can remain meaningful because of the climate, but do not confuse ambient heat with extreme mechanical stress.

---

## AUSTIN / COTA

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- mix of high-speed corners
- rapid direction changes
- slower traction sections
- meaningful straights
- requires straight-line speed plus efficient aerodynamic load
- front tyres stressed in fast first sector
- rear tyres stressed through slow-corner acceleration zones
- tyre wear meaningful
- bumps/irregular surface affect setup

COTA should be a balanced technical test.

---

## MEXICO CITY

HIGH confidence.

Characteristics:

- approximately 2,250 m above sea level
- roughly 20% reduction in air density compared with sea level
- major reduction in effective downforce
- major reduction in cooling effectiveness
- teams use very high-downforce bodywork despite low effective downforce
- cooling configuration is extremely important
- heavy braking zones
- low overall grip
- technical stadium section
- tyre confidence/temperature management matters

Cooling demand must be among the highest in the game.

Store altitude approximately 2,250 m.

Do NOT model Mexico simply as a conventional high-downforce circuit.

---

## SÃO PAULO / INTERLAGOS

MEDIUM confidence pending the 2026 event-specific technical preview.

Stable verified characteristics:

- short lap
- combination of straights and twistier infield sections
- elevation/camber changes
- bumpy surface
- balanced longitudinal and lateral tyre loads
- tyre wear/graining can matter
- weather highly variable
- rain tendency meaningful
- strong setup compromise rather than one dominant characteristic

Keep profile broadly balanced.

Do not invent extreme values without new evidence.

---

## LAS VEGAS

MEDIUM confidence pending the 2026 event-specific technical preview.

Stable verified characteristics:

- average speeds comparable to Monza
- long flat-out straights
- tight/twisty sequences between straights
- major straight-line emphasis
- strong braking requirement
- low-downforce tendencies
- cold conditions make tyre warm-up a major issue
- street surface begins low-grip and evolves significantly
- historic graining/warm-up issue is more important than inherently extreme tyre wear

Keep straight-line and braking very high.

Do not model it as a high-downforce circuit.

---

## LUSAIL

MEDIUM confidence pending the 2026 event-specific technical preview.

Stable verified characteristics:

- fast and flowing
- medium/high-speed corners dominate
- approximately 1 km main straight
- exceptionally high tyre energy
- high thermal/lateral tyre stress
- historically among the most severe circuits for tyres
- characteristics comparable in severity to Silverstone/Suzuka

High-speed aero and tyre demand should be among the highest.

---

## YAS MARINA

MEDIUM confidence pending the 2026 event-specific technical preview.

Stable verified characteristics:

- approximately 1.2 km principal straight
- slow corners at each end of major straight
- significant braking
- tricky loaded braking sequence later in lap
- technical final sector
- rear traction important
- thermal tyre degradation generally rear-biased
- mixture of flat-out and tight sections

Balanced straight-line/braking/traction profile.

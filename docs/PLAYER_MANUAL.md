# Formula Tycoon Player Manual

This is a living guide to the current prototype. Driver browsing, scouting report reviews,
contract information, calendar/circuit briefings and HQ interactions are available. Racing, a dynamic form engine, sponsors,
payroll and a complete career loop are not available yet. Planned effects are identified below.

## Drivers

Open **DRIVERS** for the starting grid, prospects and reserve/free-agent market. Open **TEAM**
to compare your current race-seat pairing. The starting organization is Wellington Racing.

Drivers have six broad evaluation categories, shown as letter grades from F to A+:

- **Talent:** speed and qualifying execution.
- **Racecraft:** racing execution and tyre management.
- **Adaptability:** handling unfamiliar cars, changing grip and conditions.
- **Composure:** consistency, discipline and handling pressure.
- **Technical Feedback:** helping an organization understand its car.
- **Marketability:** commercial profile and reach.

There is no universal driver overall rating. A driver can excel in one area and be less effective
in another. Specialties describe additional strengths; some remain unconfirmed until scouted.
Age and career stage provide context, not a promise of scripted future results.

## Driver Performance

The intended race model considers the driver, car, track, conditions, strategy and current form
together. A strong driver in an unsuitable or weak car can have poor results. A young driver can
have an exceptional season without possessing a veteran's technical experience.

The fixed 2026 starting evaluations are a starting snapshot, not a permanent career ceiling.
Adaptability, feedback and commercial value are evaluated separately from current-season pace.
For example, Sainor and Alvarro retain useful veteran strengths despite weaker starting pace
information. Future careers will be driven by Formula Tycoon outcomes rather than scripted history.

Current form is described as **EXCELLENT**, **GOOD**, **NORMAL**, **POOR** or **STRUGGLING**.
Everyone currently begins at NORMAL. Form does not evolve yet, and no race results are simulated.

## Car Fit

A talented driver may be more comfortable in one kind of car than another. Driver detail shows
an initial **Comfortable**, **Adapting** or **Challenging** estimate for the assigned car, alongside
organization familiarity. An unsigned driver has no assigned car. Familiarity and underlying
skills are separate; changing teams does not erase talent.

The current fit estimate is coarse. Track, weather and detailed handling effects are future work;
this display does not currently apply a race or development bonus.

## Technical Feedback

Experienced, technically strong drivers can have value beyond raw pace. Technical Feedback
represents that organizational contribution: car understanding, setup discussion and useful
engineering information. Veterans such as Harlton, Alvarro and Sainor can remain valuable here.

Connections to setup, simulator work and upgrade correlation are planned. Feedback does not
currently change development outcomes or generate an additional hidden bonus.

## Marketability

Marketability represents reputation, popularity, nationality and home-country appeal, regional
markets and global reach. These commercial strengths need not match on-track strengths.
Commercial information can be uncertain for incompletely scouted drivers.

Sponsors and commercial payouts are not implemented. A strong commercial grade does not currently
create income.

## Contracts

Driver detail shows annual salary in millions, the guaranteed end season and any existing team
option. Salary is an estimated annual average obligation, not a signing payment. Payroll and
performance-bonus payouts are not simulated yet.

**Guaranteed through** includes that season. **Future guaranteed years after this season** excludes
the current season: at the start of 2026 a deal through 2026 has zero future guaranteed years,
while a deal through 2030 has four. This does not mean the 2026 deal has already expired.

A **team option** is an additional season the team may activate. It is separate from the guaranteed
term until exercised. The profile shows its year, state and decision deadline. Only the owning
team can exercise it, and a conflicting seat or driver booking prevents the action. Pending
options lapse after the displayed deadline. The current Wellington starting pair has no options;
other teams' option terms can be inspected, but their decisions are not yours to make.

After the effective deal ends, the seat becomes vacant unless another contract covers it.
Automatic offseason recruitment, negotiations, buyouts and a full signing screen are future work.

**Confirmed** and **Reported** describe the starting-data source. Both create binding career
contracts with identical mechanical reliability. Newly created career contracts need no such label.
New careers use the aligned 2026 deals; imported earlier prototype careers retain their existing
signed terms and decisions.

A reserve deal is distinct from a race seat. Yuto Takase is associated with the Vortex/Veloce
system and has a Vortex reserve contract through 2026. He is outside the 22-driver starting grid
and does not appear as a third driver in the race-seat TEAM comparison.

## Scouting

Established starting drivers are well known. Prospects and reserve-market drivers may instead
show grade ranges, uncertain potential or **Unknown**. These are estimates, not extra abilities.
True hidden values are not displayed directly.

**Improve scouting report** narrows the uncertainty for your organization without changing the
driver's skills. Other teams keep their own knowledge. Confidence, the last report date and report
count show what you currently know. Report reviews are free and immediate in this prototype;
scouting staff, budgets and elapsed-time assignments are future work.

## Time & Calendar

Your career begins on **Monday, March 2, 2026**. The in-game date stays the same wherever you
play; it is separate from the real date and the clock on your device.

Development and contract deadlines use this shared career calendar. The HQ **NEW DAY** button
currently replays the morning arrivals; it does **not** move the career date forward.

Future calendar controls may advance directly to important events or deadlines, so you will not
need to click through every day. Race weekends, travel, scouting assignments and future facility
work will use the same calendar. Smart event-advance controls are not available yet. The season calendar can now be browsed
from RACE, and the HQ next-event card follows the shared career date.

## Calendar & Race Weekends

Open **RACE** to browse the 23-round starting calendar. Each card shows the round, venue,
country, weekend dates and any **SPRINT** marker. A Sprint marker identifies a shorter-format
weekend; Sprint sessions and races are not simulated yet.

The next event is highlighted. **THIS WEEKEND** includes the opening and race dates.
**COMPLETED** currently means the date has passed; it does not mean a race result was generated.
The HQ next-event card shows the same date-based schedule and countdown.

## Circuit Characteristics

Tap an event for its circuit briefing. Different tracks reward different technical strengths:
Monza and Las Vegas emphasize straight-line efficiency and braking; Monaco rewards low-speed
performance, traction and mechanical grip; Suzuka and Silverstone emphasize high-speed aero.
Sepang places broad demands on aero, tyres and cooling.

Track demands appear as LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH or VERY HIGH. These are initial game-model descriptions,
not exact measurements. Madrid's current profile reflects its confirmed mixed-speed layout and
strong tyre-energy demands. Imported earlier careers retain their original circuit profiles.

## Car Characteristics

The technical model reads the parts fitted to your car. Aero components, suspension, cooling,
power-unit performance and reliability feed different characteristics. A project still in the
factory does not improve the fitted car. Reverted upgrades do not create a hidden improvement.
Existing low-drag and cooling packages apply only where their technical conditions match.

## Car / Track Fit

Circuit briefings show your team's **EXCELLENT**, **STRONG**, **COMPETITIVE**, **MIXED**, **WEAK**
or **POOR** technical suitability, with relevant strengths and concerns. A single universal car
rating cannot describe every circuit. Upgrades may help more at some venues than others.

A hot venue can demand strong cooling without unusually high mechanical stress or tyre wear.
Cold tyre warm-up and tyre wear are different challenges; detailed warm-up is not simulated yet.

Technical suitability is only one part of eventual race performance. It excludes driver ability,
form, strategy and dynamic weather, and predicts no finishing position. It is separate from the
driver's comfort with the car shown in Driver Detail. Qualifying and races remain future work.

## Headquarters and saving

Use **HQ** to return to the working headquarters. Tap workers or departments for their cards;
**NEW DAY** restarts the workday. Navigation keeps the same live game session.

The tested save format preserves driver details, contracts and scouting information, including
older-format migration. A player-facing save/load screen and durable device storage do not exist
yet. Closing or reloading the prototype should not be treated as saving a career.

# Formula Tycoon — Product Vision

Formula Tycoon is a mobile racing-organization management game built around an evolving fictional driver universe.

The emotional hook is not driving the car. It is building a team, watching people work inside a living headquarters, developing fictional drivers, traveling to race weekends, and accumulating decades of history.

Core future loop:

HQ → DEVELOP → SCOUT → PREPARE → PACK → TRAVEL → QUALIFY → RACE → RESULTS → RETURN TO HQ

The HQ should feel alive in the style of classic tycoon/management games: a readable isometric facility, tiny employees with jobs, cars in bays, engineers at desks, logistics activity, and visible preparation before races.

The first prototype intentionally proves only the HQ presentation and worker assignment loop. The full motorsport simulation comes next.
